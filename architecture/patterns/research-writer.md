# Parallel research, one writer

Research in parallel, writing in sequence. Three agents research the same brief from different angles; one writer turns the merged findings into a draft; a verification pass challenges the draft's claims before anything is published.

## What it does

1. A brief names the topic, the audience and the allowed sources.
2. Three research agents, each with a different angle, gather findings with their sources.
3. The claims are merged and deduped.
4. One writer drafts from the claims list only, citing a source after every factual sentence.
5. A verification pass challenges every claim in the draft against its sources. It was added in July 2026 after a trial run caught claims the pipeline had let through. Fail means rewrite, not publish. The rebuild makes this step a script: every citation must exist and every quote must really be in the source.

## Why this shape

Research benefits from several views; prose does not. One writer keeps one voice. Independent industry platforms published the pieces, and a verification pass stood between every draft and the platform.

## Where it ran

Long-form articles that independent platforms chose to publish. See [the paid-media chapter](../../evidence/case-studies/02-paid-media-that-ends-in-a-human-follow-up.md) for the surrounding motion and [article-pipeline](https://github.com/nunomnferreirapt/article-pipeline) for the rebuild.
